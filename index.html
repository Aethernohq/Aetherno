Solution

Your receptionist.
Built for your business.
Always on.

AETHERNO sets up a fully automated answering service on your business line that picks up every call you miss — after hours, on weekends, while you're out on a job, or whenever you can't get to the phone.

When a call comes in and you don't answer, it routes automatically to your AETHERNO agent. The agent greets the caller naturally, finds out what they need and books the appointment directly onto your calendar. You get a notification with the caller's name, their service need, and the confirmed time.

If you pick up yourself, nothing happens. The agent only activates when you don't. It's a safety net on your existing line, not a change to how you run your business.

AETHERNO specializes in service companies. Every agent is built specifically for your business — your services, your area, your hours, your rules. You don't need to move a finger.
