Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This policy applies to the AETHERNO website (aetherno.com) and any services provided by AETHERNO. Please read it carefully. If you have questions, contact us at info@aetherno.com.

1. Who We Are

AETHERNO ("we," "us," or "our") provides AI voice automation services for businesses. We operate from Athens, Greece, and serve clients primarily in the United States. Our website is aetherno.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and business name — when you fill out a contact form, book a call via Calendly, or send us an email.
  • Communications — emails, call recordings (when you interact with our AI voice agent, Alex, as a test or during a pilot), and messages you send us.
  • Usage data — information about how you use our website, including pages visited, time spent, and browser or device information, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Payment information — if you become a paying client, billing is handled through Stripe. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you about our services
  • Set up, manage, and improve your service experience
  • Send you service-related notifications and updates (not marketing, unless you opt in)
  • Process billing and track performance metrics agreed upon in your service agreement
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve our website and services

4. AI Voice Agent Disclosure

AETHERNO deploys AI-powered voice agents ("Alex") that answer inbound calls on behalf of our clients. Calls handled by Alex may be recorded and/or transcribed for quality assurance, training, and service delivery purposes.

In compliance with the FCC's Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable state laws (including Texas, Florida, and Arizona regulations), callers interacting with Alex are or will be informed at the start of the call that they are speaking with an automated service. Our clients are responsible for ensuring their call handling practices comply with applicable laws in their jurisdiction.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve website performance and understand how visitors use our site. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Functional cookies — necessary for the website to operate correctly (e.g., remembering your cookie preference)
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors navigate our site so we can improve it

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect your experience on our site. We do not use cookies to serve advertising or sell your data to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Calendly — for scheduling calls. Subject to Calendly's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — for payment processing. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Retell AI — for AI voice agent infrastructure. Call data may be processed by Retell AI's systems.
  • Google Workspace — for email and calendar. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with our legal obligations. Contact information is retained for the duration of our business relationship and for a reasonable period afterward. Call recordings are retained as specified in client agreements. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 9).

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt-out — opt out of any marketing communications at any time

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the "sale" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at info@aetherno.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
